# K4 — Anchoring: can operator fingerprints match cell groups across batches without labels?

status: pre-registered (approved by Tom 2026-11-01)
blocked_by: none (K3, T04 ACCEPT); platform: the **Manus cloud sandbox used for T04/T04b/K3** (needs their caches)
seed: 20261014
planner_commit: 7e9a371
kind: diagnostic (feasibility of operators as anchors; no alignment, no correction)
motivated_by: Tom 2026-11-01 — test anchoring. K3 and T04c: operators separate cell types strongly (9.7×, 15.5×), and K1/K2: eigenvalue spectra agree across runs and chemistries, but operator directions and network space failed. Anchoring uses only the frame-free part.

## Purpose

In each dataset separately, cluster the cells without labels, describe each
cluster by a **frame-free operator fingerprint** (the eigenvalues of its
centroid operator), and match clusters across the two datasets by
fingerprint alone. Labels are used only to score the matches. If the
matches are right well beyond chance, operators can serve as anchors for a
group-wise alignment (anchoring + positions, or + operator directions if
O1l passes). If they are no better than chance, anchoring is dropped too.
A plain expression fingerprint (cluster mean expression, which needs shared
genes) is the baseline.

## Scope

### In scope
- **Pairs (cached inputs only, read-only, sha256 recorded; no new fits):**
  - **P1 — technical halves:** K3's P1 latents (`reports/K3/cache/p1_latents.npz`)
    and K3's 10 P1 fits (H1, H2 × seeds 20261014, 20261016–19).
  - **P2 — gene-level batch (T04 S0):** T04's `z_a`, `z_b` and S0 fits
    (A, B × the same 5 seeds).
  - **P3 — batch + biology (T04 S1):** T04's S1 latents and fits.
- **Clusters (per copy, separately):** AnnData on the copy's 30-d latent;
  `sc.pp.neighbors(n_neighbors=15, use_rep="X", random_state=20261014)`;
  `sc.tl.leiden(resolution=1.0, random_state=20261014, flavor="igraph",
  n_iterations=2, directed=False)`. Keep clusters with ≥ 30 cells
  (dropped counts recorded). Cluster ids sorted by size, descending.
- **Operator fingerprint:** per cluster `c` and seed `t`:
  `C_t = centroid_operator(z, M_t, n_cells_centroid=30)` on that cluster's
  cells (T04's / K3's implementation; float64, symmetric part);
  `λ_t = eigvalsh(C_t)` sorted descending (30 values). Fingerprint
  `f_c = mean_t λ_t`. Standardise each of the 30 coordinates by its mean
  and SD over **all clusters of both copies of the pair pooled**; distance
  = Euclidean.
- **Expression fingerprint (baseline):** per cluster, mean of the copy's
  own log-normalised expression over the 2000 genes (`genes.txt`,
  `normalize_total(1e4)`, `log1p`; for P2/P3 the T04 count matrices of that
  scenario); distance = `1 − Pearson r`.
- **Matching:** `scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment` on the distance
  matrix (rows = copy 1 clusters: H1 or A; columns = copy 2: H2 or B);
  rectangular allowed (`min(n₁, n₂)` pairs).
- **Scoring (labels used here only):** each cluster's label = majority
  coarse `cell_type` of its cells (ties: alphabetical first). A matched pair
  is **correct** if the two labels are equal. `acc` = correct pairs ÷
  pairs.
- **Chance (permutation null, operator and expression share it):** 1000
  random matchings per pair, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261400)`, pairs in
  order P1, P2, P3: if `n₁ ≤ n₂`, row `i` ↔ column `rng.permutation(n₂)[i]`;
  otherwise the transpose. `chance` = mean accuracy;
  `p_op = (1 + #{perm acc ≥ acc_op}) / 1001`.
- **Figure:** `fig_anchors.png` — for each pair, the operator distance
  matrix (rows and columns ordered by label) with the chosen matches
  marked, plus a bar chart of `acc_op`, `acc_expr` and `chance` per pair.

### Out of scope
- Any alignment or correction; other clusterings, resolutions, cluster
  sizes, fingerprints (tangent spectra, basin index, eigenvectors), seeds
  or pairs; new drift fits; the chemistry pair (fits are on the Mac).

### Gated numbers

| `acc_expr_P3` | P3 | same |
| `chance_P2` | permutation-null mean accuracy, P2 | same |

| `symmetry_max` | max median asymmetry over the fits loaded | `--stage check` |
| `k4_outcome` | `"no_anchor"`, `"weak"`, `"anchors_equal"` or `"anchors_better"` | `--stage report` |

### Decision rule
0. Validity → REVISE: `n_pairs_P1 < 8`, `n_pairs_P2 < 8`, or
   `symmetry_max > 1e-4`.
1. **no_anchor** if `p_op_P2 > 0.01` or `acc_op_P2 < chance_P2 + 0.20`.
2. **anchors_better** if `acc_op_P1 ≥ 0.80`, `acc_op_P2 ≥ 0.80` and
   `acc_op_P2 ≥ acc_expr_P2 + 0.10`.
3. **anchors_equal** if `acc_op_P1 ≥ 0.80` and `acc_op_P2 ≥ 0.80`.
4. Otherwise **weak**.
- Reading (not the outcome): P3 shows whether the interferon response
  breaks the matching of B's monocyte clusters; P1 vs P2 shows how much the
  gene-level batch costs operator vs expression anchors.

### What would change my mind
If frame-free operator fingerprints cannot match clusters across two
halves of one run or a mild gene-level batch better than chance, operators
do not identify cell groups robustly enough to anchor anything, and the
remaining operator roles are descriptive only.

## Procedure

1. `--stage check` — load inputs (sha256), symmetry of the fits.
2. `--stage cluster` — Leiden per copy; `clusters.csv` (pair, copy,
   cluster, n_cells, label, label_purity).
3. `--stage match` — fingerprints, distances, matching, scores, null;
   `matches.csv` (pair, fingerprint, row_cluster, col_cluster, distance,
   row_label, col_label, correct); `fingerprints.npz`.
4. `--stage report` — `fig_anchors.png`, `report.json`, checksums, lockfile.

`--stage all`; detached, resumable; branch `task/K4` from `main` in the same
clone; push after each stage. Minutes of compute (cached fits).
