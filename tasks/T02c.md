## Purpose

T02 and T02b could not estimate a 30-d rotation from operators at ~970
cells per dataset: too few well-estimated correspondences for 435
parameters. T02c separates the two questions.

- **Part 1 (decides):** the rotation between the v2 and v3 PCA frames is
  set by their shared gene loadings (`Q_load`, no fitting, identifiable at
  any cell count). Under `Q_load`, do the v2 and v3 **centroid operators**
  of the big clusters agree within drift-fit noise, in the top 10 PCs?
- **Part 2 (explains):** fitting a rotation from those centroid operators
  alone, in the top 10 PCs (45 parameters), does it land near `Q_load`,
  and is it reproducible?

Outcomes (Part 1): **frame agreement** → planner proposes T03 on a larger
same-sample, multi-protocol dataset; **no frame agreement** /
**inconclusive** → Tom.

## Scope

### In scope
- **Inputs (record sha256 of each):** `reports/K2/latent_pca30_a.npy`,
  `reports/K2/latent_pca30_b.npy`, `reports/K2/ops_a.npz`,
  `reports/K2/ops_b.npz`, `reports/T02b/loadings.npz`,
  `data/pbmc_v2_v3/k2_cells.csv`, `data/pbmc_v2_v3/pbmc_v2.h5ad`,
  `data/pbmc_v2_v3/pbmc_v3_thin.h5ad`, `reports/T01d/genes.txt`. A =
  matched v2, B = matched v3-thin, K2's cell order and coarse
  `cell_type` labels. No new PCA for the separate frames, no new labels.
- **Clusters:** coarse types with **≥ 100 cells in both A and B**, sorted
  (expected: B cells, Monocytes, T cells). Weights
  `w_k = min(n_A,k, n_B,k) / Σ min(n_A,k, n_B,k)`.
- **Drift fits:** as T02 — seeds 20261014, 20261016, 20261017, 20261018,
  20261019 per dataset on K2's latents with K2's config (ten fits);
  `J` float64; `M_c = ½(J_c + J_cᵀ)`; K2 reproduction check on the
  seed-20261014 restricted eigenvalues.
- **Centroid operator:** `scjdo2.compare.centroid_operator(z_k, M_k,
  n_cells_centroid=30)` — mean of `M_c` over the 30 cells of cluster `k`
  nearest its latent centroid. `C_k^A`, `C_k^B` from the seed-20261014 fits.
- **Top-10 block:** for a 30×30 matrix `X`, `X₁₀ = X[:10, :10]` (first 10
  PCs of the frame it is expressed in).
- **`Q_load`:** from `loadings.npz`, `G = W_B · W_Aᵀ`; `U, σ, Vᵀ = svd(G)`;
  `Q_load = U Vᵀ` (30×30). `Q_load₁₀ = polar(G[:10, :10])` (via its SVD,
  `U₁₀ V₁₀ᵀ`).
- **Residual:** `resid(X^A ↦ X^B) = sqrt( Σ_k w_k ‖X_k^A − X_k^B‖_F² /
  Σ_k w_k ‖X_k^B‖_F² )`.
- **Part 1:**
  - `resid_identity_10` = resid of `C_k^A[:10,:10]` vs `C_k^B[:10,:10]`;
  - `resid_load_10` = resid of `(Q_load C_k^A Q_loadᵀ)[:10,:10]` vs
    `C_k^B[:10,:10]`;
  - **null:** for A, then B, and each `s_b` in (20261016, 20261017,
    20261018, 20261019): `rng_null = numpy.random.default_rng(20261022)`
    (one generator, this order); per cluster (sorted) `permutation`, halves
    of `⌊n_k/2⌋`; centroid operator of half 1 from the seed-20261014 fit,
    of half 2 from the `s_b` fit; resid of their top-10 blocks (same frame,
    no rotation). `resid_null_10` = median of the eight;
  - `frame_ratio_load = resid_load_10 / resid_null_10`.
- **Part 2:**
  - rotation `R` (10×10) minimising
    `L(R) = Σ_k w_k ‖R C_k^A[:10,:10] Rᵀ − C_k^B[:10,:10]‖_F² / Σ_k w_k ‖C_k^B[:10,:10]‖_F²`;
    `R = R₀ · matrix_exp(S − Sᵀ)`, torch float64 CPU, LBFGS as T02;
    **20 starts:** `R₀ = I`, `R₀ = Q_load₁₀`, then
    `ortho_group.rvs(dim=10, random_state=20261021 + i)` for `i = 1…18`;
    `R̂` = lowest final loss; `resid_opfit_10 = sqrt(L(R̂))`;
  - `q_op_vs_load_10 = ‖R̂ − Q_load₁₀‖_F / sqrt(20)`;
  - **split reproducibility:** `rng_split = numpy.random.default_rng(20261023)`;
    per cluster (sorted), A then B, `permutation`, halves of `⌊n_k/2⌋`;
    centroid operators of each half (seed-20261014 fits); fit `R̂₁` on the
    half-1 pairs and `R̂₂` on the half-2 pairs (same estimator, same 20
    starts); `r_split = ‖R̂₁ − R̂₂‖_F / sqrt(20)`;
  - **seed reproducibility:** fit `R̂_s` from centroid operators of the
    seed-20261016 fits (all cells); `r_seed = ‖R̂ − R̂_s‖_F / sqrt(20)`.
- **Views:** T02's view 1 (joint PCA, uncorrected) and view 2 (separate
  PCAs, no rotation), plus **view 3 = separate PCAs with `z_A' = z_A ·
  Q_loadᵀ`**; T02's UMAP parameters and `mix` / `sil` scores (silhouette on
  the K2 tested types).

### Out of scope
- Rotations or operators beyond the top 10 PCs in Part 2; any other
  cluster rule, operator summary, estimator, weighting, threshold or seed.
- Using `R̂` (Part 2) to move cells; fine labels; other datasets; local maps.

### Decision rule (Part 1 decides)
Top to bottom; first match decides. Stops reported as `"status": "stopped"`.
0. **Validity:** `n_cells_a ≠ n_cells_b`, `k2_repro_max_abs > 1e-5` or
   `symmetry_max > 1e-4` → **REVISE**. `n_clusters < 3` →
   **inconclusive (stop)**.
1. `frame_ratio_load ≤ 1.5` → **frame agreement**.
2. `frame_ratio_load > 3.0` → **no frame agreement**.
3. Otherwise → **inconclusive**.

**Part 2 reading (pre-registered, not the outcome):**
- the operators alone recover the gene-anchored rotation if
  `n_starts_near_best_R ≥ 2` **and** `q_op_vs_load_10 ≤ 0.25` **and**
  `r_split ≤ 0.25`;
- `r_seed` is reported beside `r_split` as the drift-fit share of that
  variability;
- otherwise: at this cell count, operators validate a rotation but do not
  estimate it.

**Views reading (not the outcome):** `mix_sep_load > mix_sep_noA` and
`sil_sep_load ≥ 0.9 × sil_sep_noA`.

### What would change my mind
`frame_ratio_load > 3.0` means that even with the frames lined up by the
shared genes, the v2 and v3 wells of the same cell types point in
different directions, beyond drift-fit noise.

## Procedure

1. `t02c_run.py --stage fits` — ten fits (kept locally); K2 reproduction;
   `centroid_ops.npz` (arrays: `C_a`, `C_b` (seeds, clusters, 30, 30) for
   all five seeds; `null_h1`, `null_h2` (8, clusters, 30, 30);
   `split_a`, `split_b` (2, clusters, 30, 30); `clusters`, `w`).
2. `t02c_run.py --stage part1` — `part1.csv` (quantity, value), `null.csv`.
3. `t02c_run.py --stage part2` — `R.npy`, `R_split.npy` (2, 10, 10),
   `R_seed.npy`, `starts_R.csv`.
4. `t02c_run.py --stage views` — `umap_views.npz`, `fig_umap.png`
   (3 views × coloured by dataset / cell type, panel titles with `mix` and
   `sil`), `fig_frames.png` (per cluster: absolute cosine between matched
   top-10 eigenvectors of `(Q_load C^A Q_loadᵀ)₁₀` and `C^B₁₀`, and for no
   rotation).
5. `t02c_run.py --stage report` — `report.json`, checksums, lockfile.

`t02c_run.py` must accept `--stage all`. `centroid_ops.npz` is saved so the
planner can recompute Parts 1 and 2 from it.
