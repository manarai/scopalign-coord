## Purpose

T02c Part 1 summarises each cluster by its **centroid operator** (mean over
the 30 cells nearest the latent centroid). The axiom is about wells, whose
natural reference point is the **floor** (energy minimum = density peak,
since `E = −log p` with β = 1). A single argmin is fragile, so this task
uses the **basin-floor operator**: the mean operator over the **30 cells of
the cluster with the lowest learned energy**. Everything else is T02c Part 1
unchanged, so the two summaries can be compared directly. Written before
T02c's result is known.

Outcomes as T02c: **frame agreement** / **no frame agreement** /
**inconclusive**. Reported beside T02c; neither replaces the other.

## Scope

### In scope
- **Inputs (record sha256 of each):** exactly T02c's, plus
  `reports/T02c/report.json` (read for the comparison number only).
- **Clusters, weights, drift fits, K2 reproduction, `Q_load`, top-10 block,
  residual definition, null procedure (`default_rng(20261022)`, same order),
  Part 1 quantities and thresholds:** exactly as `tasks/T02c.md` Part 1.
- **Basin-floor operator** (replaces the centroid operator everywhere in
  Part 1, including the null): for cluster `k` and a given drift fit,
  `e = drift.energy(z_k)` on that cluster's cells (with that fit);
  `F_k` = the 30 cells with the smallest `e` (ties by cell order,
  `numpy.argsort(e, kind="stable")[:30]`); operator `= mean of M_c over F_k`.
  In the null, half 1 uses the seed-20261014 fit's energies and operators,
  half 2 the `s_b` fit's.
- **Overlap diagnostic:** per cluster, fraction of the 30 basin-floor cells
  (seed 20261014) that are also among the 30 cells nearest the centroid;
  mean over clusters and datasets.

### Out of scope
- T02c Part 2, views, or any rotation fitting; any other floor size,
  operator summary, threshold or seed; other datasets.

### Decision rule
T02c's Part 1 rule, unchanged: validity (as T02c; `n_clusters < 3` →
inconclusive (stop)); `frame_ratio_load ≤ 1.5` → **frame agreement**;
`> 3.0` → **no frame agreement**; otherwise **inconclusive**.
`floor_vs_centroid` and `floor_centroid_overlap` are reported, never
decisive. If T02c stopped before Part 1, `floor_vs_centroid` is omitted and
the report says so.

### What would change my mind
If the centroid version agrees (T02c) but the floor version does not, or
the reverse, the conclusion depends on where in the well the operator is
read, and neither summary alone can carry the axiom.
