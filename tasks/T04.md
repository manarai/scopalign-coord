# T04 — Known batch + known biology: which methods remove the batch and keep the biology?

## Purpose

Test the project's hypothesis directly: **does operator-level correction
keep a real biological difference that cell-moving methods remove, and
does the operator mismatch left after correction flag it?**

Semi-synthetic data with both answers known: real PBMC cells split into
two halves (K0); half B gets (1) a **realistic, non-rotational batch effect**
in gene space and (2) a **known biological change** (an interferon
response) in half of its monocytes. Six methods are scored on batch
removal, biology retention, and (for rotation methods) the operator flag.

## Platform

Manus **cloud sandbox**, after C0 has ACCEPTed there (same sandbox,
`~/scopalign-cloud`). All T04 numbers come from the cloud; none is compared
with a Mac number (C0 rule). Long stages run detached and resumable;
push `reports/T04` to `task/T04` after each stage so a sandbox reset loses
nothing.

## Scope

### In scope
- **Inputs (record sha256 / fingerprints):** `data/pbmc_10k_v3/pbmc_10k_v3.h5ad`
  and `k0_halves.csv` as rebuilt by C0 (QC-passing cells; H1 = **A**,
  H2 = **B**; coarse `cell_type`), `reports/K0/genes.txt` (2000 genes).
  Raw counts of these genes.
- **Biology (B only, scenario S1):** module `G_bio` = the interferon genes
  in `genes.txt`: ISG15, IFI6, IFIT1, IFIT2, IFIT3, MX1, MX2, OAS1, OAS2,
  OAS3, OASL, RSAD2, IFI44, IFI44L, HERC5, STAT1, IRF7, XAF1, LY6E, EPSTI1,
  IFITM3, PLSCR1 (22; validity: all present). Responders: a random half of
  B's `Monocytes` (`default_rng(20261100).choice`, without replacement,
  sorted). For responder `c`, gene `g ∈ G_bio`:
  `x'_cg = x_cg + Poisson(4 · (μ_g + 0.1) · n_c / n̄)`, with `μ_g` = mean raw
  count of `g` in A's monocytes, `n_c` the cell's total count, `n̄` the mean
  total count of B (`default_rng(20261102)`). Other cells/genes unchanged.
- **Batch effect (all B cells, both scenarios, after biology):** per gene
  `b_g = exp(N(0, 0.5²))` (`default_rng(20261101)`, genes in `genes.txt`
  order) times a depth factor 0.7; `x''_cg ~ Poisson(x'_cg · b_g · 0.7)`.
  A is resampled once, `x_cg ~ Poisson(x_cg)` (same generator, after B), so
  both halves carry one resampling.
- **Scenarios:** **S0** batch only (no responders changed; the same cells
  are tracked as "pseudo-responders"); **S1** batch + biology. An
  **oracle** for S1: A resampled + B with biology but no batch effect,
  embedded by joint PCA (biology as it would look with no batch).
- **Per-copy latents:** each half on its own: subset to `genes.txt`,
  `normalize_total(1e4)`, `log1p`, `PCA(30, svd_solver="full",
  random_state=20261014)` (as K0).
- **Methods (each a 30-d embedding of all A and B cells):**
  1. `none` — `[z_A ; z_B]`, separate frames.
  2. `joint` — joint PCA of the stacked log matrices (as T05a).
  3. `harmony` — as T05a (`harmonypy==0.0.10`, same call and seed).
  4. `scvi` — as T05a (counts, `n_latent=30`, `max_epochs=400`, seed).
  5. `gene_rot` — `[z_A · Q_loadᵀ ; z_B]`, `Q_load = polar(W_B W_Aᵀ)`.
  6. `scopalign` — `[z_A · Q̂ᵀ ; z_B]`, `Q̂` = O1b's E5 (flow + curvature,
     all cells, no pairing or labels): 5 drift fits per copy (seeds
     20261014, 20261016–19, O1g's drift config), O1g's clean starts (20),
     LBFGS as O1g, start workers ≤ 3.
- **Scores per method and scenario:**
  - `mix_bg` — batch removal on non-responder cells (S0: all cells except
    pseudo-responders): T02c's `mix` (30 nearest other cells, ÷ 0.5).
  - `bio_keep` — responder distinctness: mean over responders of the
    fraction of their 30 nearest neighbours that are responders, rescaled
    `(P − p₀)/(1 − p₀)`, `p₀ = n_resp / (n_mono_A + n_mono_B)`.
    `retention = bio_keep / bio_keep_oracle` (S1). In S0 the same score on
    pseudo-responders (`bio_false`, should be ≈ 0).
  - `sil` — silhouette on coarse types with ≥ 50 cells in both halves.
- **Operator flag** (methods with a frame map: `none`, `gene_rot`,
  `scopalign`): groups = coarse types with ≥ 100 cells in both halves, with
  B's monocytes split into responders and non-responders (S1; S0:
  pseudo-split). Per group, centroid operators of A (rotated by the
  method's `Q`) vs B, seed-20261014 fits, full 30×30 residual ÷ refit null
  (T02c's procedure, `default_rng(20261022)`, seeds 20261014 vs
  20261016–19, per group) → `flag_ratio[group]`.
- **Views:** UMAP per method (S1 and S0), coloured by half, by cell type,
  and responders highlighted; titles with `mix_bg`, `retention` /
  `bio_false`. Bar chart of `flag_ratio` per group for the three rotation
  methods.

### Out of scope
- Other effect sizes, modules, cell types or seeds; tuning any method;
  using labels or responder identity in any estimate (they are used only
  to construct the data and to score).

### Decision rule
0. Validity: `n_bio_genes ≠ 22`, `bio_keep_oracle < 0.3` (the biology is not
   visible even without a batch) or `symmetry_max > 1e-4` → REVISE.
1. Per method: **removes batch** if `mix_bg ≥ 0.80`; **keeps biology** if
   `retention ≥ 0.8`; **no false biology** if `bio_false ≤ 0.1`.
2. Operator flag **works** for a rotation method if `flag_resp ≥ 2.0`,
   `flag_other_max ≤ 1.5` and `flag_s0_max ≤ 1.5`.
3. `t04_outcome`:
   - **supported** if `scopalign` removes batch, keeps biology, no false
     biology, **and** either (a) at least one of `harmony`, `scvi` removes
     batch but does not keep biology, or (b) its operator flag works;
   - **not_supported** if `scopalign` does not keep biology, or if it
     removes batch less well than both Harmony and scVI (`mix_bg` lower by
     > 0.1) **and** its flag does not work;
   - otherwise **mixed**.

### What would change my mind
If Harmony and scVI keep the interferon response as well as scOPAlign and
the operator flag does not single out the responders, operator-level
correction adds nothing on this kind of biology; the project's claim would
need a different kind of biological difference (or fail).

   `report.json`, checksums, lockfile.
